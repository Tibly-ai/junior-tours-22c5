New York City

By virtue of its dense, diverse population and vibrant cultural and economic life, New York City stands alone as the world's most exhilarating destination. From the moment you arrive, you will feel its energy and enjoy its wonders. Conveniently located near the heart of the action, Junior Tours, a leading educational student travel company, has been guiding groups to NYC for over three decades.

New York City Landmarks

Statue of Liberty

This iconic symbol of freedom was a gift of friendship from the people of France in 1886. Made of copper, Lady Liberty holds a book inscribed July 4, 1776 in one hand and a torch of freedom in the other.

Central Park

Central Park is the first public park built in America. Across its 850-plus acres, popular stops include Strawberry Fields and Belvedere Castle.

Times Square

Known as the Crossroads of the World, Times Square dazzles with billboards, theaters, and constant crowds day and night.

Empire State Building

Standing 102 stories tall, the Empire State Building was the world's tallest office building for more than 40 years. The 1931 Art Deco tower offers views of 80 miles on a clear day.

St. Patrick's Cathedral

Completed in 1879, this Neo-Gothic masterpiece has long stood as a cornerstone of Catholic life in the United States.

Rockefeller Center

This 19-building complex is the heart of Midtown, home to the famous ice rink, the Rainbow Room, and the annual Christmas tree.

United Nations

The headquarters of the World Organization sits on an 18-acre site on the East side of Manhattan. Visitors explore historic chambers, learn how global decisions are made, and view exhibits on peacekeeping and sustainability.

Federal Hall

At a time when New York City was the nation's capital, it was here that George Washington took the oath of office as the first President.

Wall Street

This narrow stretch of Lower Manhattan remains the symbolic center of American finance, home to the New York Stock Exchange.

Grand Central Terminal

Constructed of glass and steel, the 100-foot by 650-foot structure rivaled the Eiffel Tower and Crystal Palace as one of the great engineering achievements of the 19th century.

Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum

Explore the historic aircraft carrier USS Intrepid, view iconic aircraft, and see the Space Shuttle Enterprise up close.

9/11 Memorial & Museum

Twin reflecting pools mark the footprints of the Twin Towers, honoring the nearly 3,000 lives lost on September 11, 2001.
