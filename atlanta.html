Atlanta

Atlanta is a city where American history, modern industry, and Southern culture all come together. From its beginnings as a railroad crossroads to its role in the Civil Rights Movement and its place today in business and media, Atlanta gives student groups a trip that's both meaningful and fun. Groups can explore Centennial Olympic Park, the Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park, and the World of Coca-Cola, along with plenty of options for hands-on learning and Southern hospitality.
