New Orleans

New Orleans isn't just a destination, it's a mood. The city's French and Spanish influences, Cajun and Creole flavors, and deep musical roots come together into an atmosphere that feels one of a kind. Whether your group is walking through the French Quarter or gliding by riverboat through the bayou, students leave with stories they'll tell for years.

NOLA is known for jazz on every corner, beignets at Cafe du Monde, and history that stretches from the Louisiana Purchase to the birth of American music. It's a rich, hands-on classroom for any student group.
