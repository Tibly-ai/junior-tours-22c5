STARS Performance Programs & Educational Tours

Beyond the fun, Universal Studios has invested heavily in youth and educational programming that can turn your trip into a real learning experience. Performing arts groups can perform live in the parks for hundreds of guests, or take part in hands-on workshops with industry professionals covering skills like improvisation, vocal technique, or behind-the-scenes production work.

Dance Workshop

Dance teams and musical theater programs work directly with a Universal choreographer to learn original choreography inspired by Universal's movies, TV shows, and parades. Students gain insight into performance quality, stage presence, rehearsal technique, and what it takes to bring high-energy routines to life on a professional stage.

Choir / Vocal Workshop

Bring a piece and refine it with a working vocal professional, or learn techniques for projection, harmony, and performance presence used across the Universal parks.

FAQs About Student Trips to Universal Orlando

How far in advance should we plan this trip?

We recommend planning 8 to 12 months out, so there's time for administrator approval, promoting the trip to families, spreading out payments, and locking in hotels and educational activities.

What's the ideal group size, and do trip leaders handle money from students?

Groups of any size work, and Junior Tours handles all payment collection directly from families, so trip leaders never manage the money themselves.

"Literally hundreds of my students have made lifelong memories on trips created by Junior Tours over the years. We feel like part of the family. The best part is knowing they had everything handled. That's why I've been touring with them for over 20 years. I feel very confident they can take care of anything, anytime, anywhere."

Best Time of Year for Student Trips to Orlando

  • September to February: Great for budget-friendly groups that want fewer crowds and to escape cold winters at home.
  • March to June: Ideal for groups traveling over spring break or celebrating the end of the school year.

Fun Facts & Statistics

Orlando is one of the most visited cities in the world, ranking alongside major global destinations for tourism, thanks in large part to its theme parks and year-round warm weather.
