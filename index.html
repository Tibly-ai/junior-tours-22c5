FAQs About Booking a Student Trip

How far in advance should I start planning?

Most groups book 8 to 10 months in advance to lock in the best dates and pricing, but we can put together a trip in as little as 3 to 4 months if you're flexible. The sooner you start, the more options you'll have.

How do I pick my travel dates?

Your coordinator will walk through your school calendar with you and suggest dates that avoid conflicts with testing, holidays, and other school events.

Do I need approval from my principal or administrators?

Most schools require sign-off before a trip is finalized. We provide the paperwork and details you need to get that approval quickly.
