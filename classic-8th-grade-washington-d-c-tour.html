Washington D.C. 8th Grade Tradition

A Signature Experience for Middle School Students in Our Nation's Capital

For 8th grade students across this country, a trip to Washington, D.C. marks the capstone of middle school. Every spring, more than a million students take part in this rite of passage. Days on the bus, inside jokes at the memorials, and late nights in the hotel hallway become memories that stick for years. Junior Tours has been building these trips since our founders were classroom teachers themselves, and we know what makes a D.C. trip work.

Sample Washington D.C. Itinerary

Day 1: Departure from your school (or local airport, if flying)

1:00PMArrive in Washington D.C., meet your tour escort, visit Arlington National Cemetery, watch the Changing of the Guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, and see the gravesites of the Kennedy brothers and the Space Shuttle Challenger crew.
3:30PMVisit the Iwo Jima Marine Corps Memorial, Air Force Memorial, and 9/11 Pentagon Memorial.

Day 2

8:00AMBreakfast at the hotel.
10:00AMTour the U.S. Capitol Building, plus the Supreme Court and Library of Congress.
12:30PMLunch at group's expense. Visit Smithsonian museums on the National Mall: African American History and Culture, Air & Space, American History, Natural History, and/or the National Gallery of Art.
4:30PMVisit the Daniel Chester French monuments and surrounding grounds.

Day 3

8:00AMBreakfast at the hotel, then check out.
10:00AMVisit the National Archives to see the Declaration of Independence, the U.S. Constitution, and the Bill of Rights.
11:30AMVisit Ford's Theatre and the Petersen House.
1:00PMLunch at group's expense.
2:00PMVisit The People's House: A White House Experience.
3:00PMRide down Embassy Row and past the Washington National Cathedral.
4:00PMApproximate time of departure home.

Optional Extension: 4-Day Package

Day 3 Evening

5:30PMDinner.
7:00PMVisit the rooftop of the Kennedy Center for views over Washington, then time in Georgetown.
9:30PMReturn to the hotel; security monitors the hallways overnight.

Day 4

8:00AMBreakfast at the hotel and check out.
10:00AMVisit Mount Vernon, George Washington's home. Lunch at group's expense.
2:00PMDeparture for home.
