Our Story

Founded in 1976 by Marty Abrams, a former Brooklyn school teacher, Junior Tours began with a simple belief: travel can open a young person's world in ways a textbook never will. Marty started this company with a classroom full of students, a vision, and the determination to give them experiences they'd remember for years.

From that first trip, the mission stayed the same: plan trips the way a teacher would want them planned, with care, attention to detail, and a real person on the phone when you need one.
