Orlando

Known as the "Theme Park Capital of the World," Orlando draws visitors of every age with its famous attractions and endless energy. But Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando offer more than thrill rides. Both parks give student groups real learning environments tied to a range of academic interests.

Performing arts groups can workshop with professionals, take backstage tours, and see how large-scale entertainment gets built from the ground up. Science and engineering students can dig into the mechanics behind rides and attractions. History and cultural studies students find plenty to explore too, from EPCOT's World Showcase to the storytelling craft behind every themed land.

Our Orlando itineraries mix classic park days with focused academic sessions, so students come home with more than souvenirs.
